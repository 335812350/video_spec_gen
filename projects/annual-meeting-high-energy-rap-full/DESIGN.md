# Design

## Style Prompt

Source-led cinematic edit. Preserve the original film frame, subtitles, stage lighting, audience reactions, and performance rhythm. No added graphic layer competes with the footage.

## Colors

- #000000: letterbox and render background
- Source footage palette: preserved

## Typography

- None added; source subtitles remain part of the footage.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not add BGM, voiceover, or sound effects.
- Do not add a second subtitle layer.
- Do not split this source segment into a montage.
- Do not crop the original 1920x800 frame.
