# Industrial Night Editorial

## Style Prompt

A restrained Chinese film-commentary treatment: deep charcoal stage, paper-white type, industrial muted red for error and evidence, full-width source footage contained inside a 16:9 frame, and sparse editorial overlays. The visual voice should feel observant and human, never promotional or sensational.

## Colors

- \`#0B0D0F\` - stage black
- \`#F3EFE7\` - paper-white primary text
- \`#D77464\` - evidence and contradiction accent
- \`rgba(243, 239, 231, .28)\` - quiet divider line

## Typography

- Microsoft YaHei for Chinese UI labels and captions
- Georgia for chapter cards and closing statements

## What NOT to Do

- No red-and-black clickbait layouts, arrows, or reaction memes.
- No gradients, decorative blobs, or card stacks.
- No BGM-driven beat cutting or noisy kinetic typography.
- No cropped source footage that removes the original widescreen framing.
- No global footage wash, full-screen black mask, vignette, blur, glow, or text shadow; chapter titles and captions sit directly over the footage.
